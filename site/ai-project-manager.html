Who it is for

Built for four kinds of people. See if you are one.

This is for you if

  • You are a fresher or student looking to break into tech or corporate roles without prior project management experience
  • You are switching from non-tech roles, customer support, sales or operations into project management
  • You are returning after a career gap and want to refresh your skills, learn modern tools and re-enter with confidence
  • You already manage projects or lead a team and want to upgrade to AI-driven workflows and modern tools

This is not for you if

  • You are looking for a shortcut to a high-paying job
  • You cannot commit time consistently over the 16-week core curriculum

Questions, answered

Questions about AI Project Management.

General questions about LeepX, refunds and payments are answered on the main FAQ.

Do I need prior project management or tech experience?

No. The program starts with the fundamentals and is designed for freshers, career switchers and working professionals alike.

When are the live sessions? What if I miss one?

On weekends, eight of them across the 16 weeks, so your weekday schedule stays untouched. Every session is recorded, so if you miss one you can watch it later and the community Q&A keeps you on track.

What happens in weeks 9 to 12?

You pick a track. Track A is creative, agency and operations: workflow automation, creative pipelines and brand governance. Track B is technical, corporate and SaaS: budget modelling, translating tech, launches and releases. Both end in capstone milestone 3.

Will this program help me get a job?

We do not guarantee job placement. The program equips you with practical frameworks, modern tools, portfolio projects and CV strategies to prepare for and apply to relevant project management roles.

What is the post-program support?

Four extra weeks of guidance after the 16-week curriculum ends, while you apply, network and interview.

Will I have access to the materials after the program ends?

Yes. You get lifetime access to the portal, lessons, templates and frameworks.

Can I pay in instalments?

Yes. An instalment payment option is available at checkout.
