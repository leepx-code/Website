Objections, handled

Every fear you have? We handle it, module by module.

I have no VA experience.
Straight answerMost students start from zero. From Module 3 you are producing real work, and by Module 10 you hold a portfolio built from your own assignments. You enter through proof, not years of experience.
I have a full-time job. No time.
Straight answerThe cohort is self-paced and needs about 5 to 7 hours a week. Every live session is recorded. Join any day, start the same day, and move at whatever speed your week allows.
Isn’t VA work just data entry?
Straight answerNo. Modern VAs run inboxes for seven-figure founders, manage launches for creators and coordinate teams across time zones. Data entry is the bottom rung. We teach you to skip it.
Will I have to work night shifts?
Straight answerNo. Most VA work is async over Slack, email and Notion. 7 to 9 AM or 9 to 11 PM IST overlaps comfortably with the US East Coast, and plenty of clients never need live hours at all.

Questions, answered

Questions about the VA program.

General questions about LeepX, refunds and payments are answered on the main FAQ.

When does the cohort start?

It is an ongoing cohort. You can join any day and start immediately, with access to every module from day one.

Do I need prior experience?

No. Many students start from zero. If you have done admin, ops, HR, customer service or project work, you are further ahead than you think.

Will I actually practise, or is it just lessons?

You submit 14 assignments, each with a brief, a template and a fully worked example: a manager persona, SOPs, a project tracker, AI tool briefs, your CV, your portfolio and a real outreach pitch. Every one doubles as a portfolio piece.

How many live sessions are there?

Seven with Shreya: an onboarding kickoff, five live Q&As where your submitted questions are answered on screen, and a graduation session. All recorded.

How much AI is in the course?

AI runs through the whole program, with a dedicated block in Module 5: ChatGPT and Claude for daily work, AI in Docs and Sheets, choosing and pitching AI tools for clients, and building your own prompt library.

Do I have to work US night shifts?

No. Most VA work is async over Slack, email and Notion. Early mornings between 7 and 9 AM IST, or evenings between 9 and 11 PM IST, overlap comfortably with the US East Coast.

I am already a VA. Is this for me?

If you are stuck at ₹15,000 to ₹25,000 a month and want international clients and premium positioning, yes. If you are already earning ₹50,000 and above from international clients, the early modules may feel basic.

What is the difference between the VA and SMM programs?

SMM is running social media for clients: strategy, content and analytics. VA is running operations for clients: inbox, calendar, projects, research and executive support. Organised and process-minded suits VA. Creative and content-driven suits SMM.

Do I need a Mac?

No. Any working laptop with reliable internet is fine, Windows included. All the tools are cloud-based.
