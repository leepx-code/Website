  • You are a student or a fresh graduate

Questions, answered

Questions about the masterclass.

Do I need prior experience?

No. The session is beginner-friendly and designed for anyone who wants to build a global career, whether you are a student, a fresher or switching lanes after years in another field.

Will I get the recording?

Yes. The recording stays available for 3 days after the session, so you can revisit anything you missed. That said, attending live is where the real value sits: you can ask questions and get answers in the room.

Will this masterclass help me get a job?

This masterclass will not guarantee a job, but it will give you the roadmap, tools and strategies to build an industry-ready profile and confidently apply for global opportunities.

What exactly do I need on the day?

Two uninterrupted hours, a notebook, and your current CV or LinkedIn open so you can apply things as we go.
