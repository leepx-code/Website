How to use
  1. Make the link here, then shorten it at bit.ly if it needs to look clean. Shortening keeps the tracking.
  2. Put the short link in the reel's pinned comment, your bio, or the DM auto-reply.
  3. Read results in GA4: Reports → Acquisition → Traffic acquisition, change the dropdown to Session campaign. One row per reel.
