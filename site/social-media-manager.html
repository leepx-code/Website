Questions, answered

Questions about the SMM cohort.

General questions about LeepX, refunds and payments are answered on the main FAQ.

I’m a fresher with no experience. Can I join?

Yes. The cohort is built around manufacturing proof: from Module 3 you’re producing real content, and by Module 9 you have a portfolio made from your own work. Freshers enter through proof, not years of experience.

Will I actually get to practise, or is it just lessons?

You practise every week. Every module ends with hands-on work on real brands: content calendars, hooks and scripts, a full content batch, a strategy doc, a brand audit and a monthly report. You submit it, we give feedback on it. Nothing here is a quiz.

Will I have a portfolio and case studies by the end?

Yes, and that is the point of the ten weeks. You produce 10+ real projects during the cohort, write each one up as a case study, and assemble them into a one-link portfolio in Module 9, which gets reviewed live before you send it anywhere.

Will I learn to use AI for social media?

Yes. Module 7 is a full AI module: the tool stack, prompt systems for research, scripting and repurposing, AI-assisted dashboards and automations, and a clear line on where AI stops and your judgement starts.

Do I need to be good at design or video editing?

No. Modules 2 and 3 teach the bare-minimum stack (Canva templates, CapCut, native editors) that covers 90% of client work. You’re hired for the system, not for being a designer.

Will you help me find clients or a job?

We do not commit a job. What we commit to: by Module 10 you are job-ready, with a portfolio, a positioned CV and a practised pitch. Launch Week is where we share the strategies for applying, pitching and outreach, live, and you keep the outreach pack, the Opportunity Board and feedback on every application asset you build.
